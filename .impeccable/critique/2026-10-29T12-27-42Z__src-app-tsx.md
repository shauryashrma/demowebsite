## Overall Impression

The initial page already had a strong palette and a useful menu-to-community story, but the large overlay headline and centered intro shifted the first screen away from Flora’s photo-led editorial rhythm. The most important opportunity was to let the image lead and move the copy below it.

## What’s Working

- Warm off-white, deep brown and botanical colors support the intended café mood.
- The homepage includes the researched section sequence and clear navigation into About, Events, Shop and Blog.
- The manual carousel controls and restrained transitions add motion without replacing basic navigation.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Hero composition and image crop** — The initial hero placed oversized copy over a full-bleed image and used a close drink crop. Flora’s reference shows an inset, wide lifestyle image with no overlay headline, then editorial copy underneath. This changed both the first-screen hierarchy and image proportion. **Fix:** Keep the carousel controls on the image, remove the overlay copy, use an inset wide crop and place the brand intro below. **Applied:** Done; current slide uses a wide inset image, with the first slide desaturated.
2. **[P1] Intro geometry and vertical rhythm** — The initial 525px centered intro with a circular stamp added a large pause before the menu. The reference uses a compact split headline-and-paragraph block immediately after the hero. **Fix:** Use a two-column editorial intro with tighter vertical spacing. **Applied:** Done; mobile stacks the two columns.
3. **[P2] Header hierarchy** — The initial green utility strip and centered slogan did not match the reference’s red announcement bar and cream navigation row. The local wordmark is still a typeset mark rather than the reference’s script signature. **Fix:** Rebalance the utility bar, brand note and navigation in two clear horizontal bands. **Applied:** Updated the strip to restrained floral red and added a small brand note; wordmark artwork remains a brand-specific difference.
4. **[P2] Menu image composition** — Three narrow staggered images made the menu section busier than the reference’s single dominant menu image. **Fix:** Use one broad image before the categorized menu list. **Applied:** Done; removed two eager image elements and widened the remaining photo.
5. **[P2] Small supporting type** — Labels, captions and footer copy fell to 6–9px, and the detector measured low contrast in several secondary text areas. These details are hard to read, especially on mobile. **Fix:** Raise key caption, menu, journal, newsletter and footer text sizes and improve newsletter text contrast. **Applied:** Done for those named areas; a full post-fix detector rescan was not run.
