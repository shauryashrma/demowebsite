# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local café visitors who need to check the menu, opening hours, and location before deciding to visit.

## Product Purpose

Provide a clear, dependable website for OM Freedom Cafe so nearby visitors can find practical visit information and learn what the café offers.

## Positioning

Open decision. The owner has not confirmed the café’s distinctive promise or preferred description. Do not turn public review summaries into brand claims.

## Operating Context

The user identified OM Freedom Cafe via a Google share link. Its public listing places it in Laxman Jhula, Rishikesh, Uttarakhand. Exact business hours remain unconfirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This is an existing React, TypeScript, and Vite web project. The requested site scope includes menu, café information, events, merchandise, editorial content, newsletter, and contact. The current Morrow Café implementation is a draft concept, not confirmed business content. Do not imply that OM Freedom Cafe offers events, merchandise, or newsletter signup until the owner confirms them. No booking, checkout, or mailing-list service is connected.

## Brand Commitments

The target business name is **OM Freedom Cafe**, identified by the user’s supplied link. No brand voice, logo, visual assets, or other identity rules have been confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied [Google share link](https://share.google/4O0G5MSlT7HeSrMyn), which resolves to the OM Freedom Cafe public listing.
- The listing gives the address as Laxman Jhula, Rishikesh, Uttarakhand 249302.
- Public listings describe a broad café menu, including Continental, Israeli, North Indian, fast-food, beverage, and dessert categories. Treat these as leads for owner verification, not approved menu copy.
- Public summaries mention views of the Ganga. This has not been confirmed by the owner as a brand promise.
- Exact hours and contact numbers differ across public listings. No official website, approved menu, verified hours, or owner-confirmed contact details have been supplied.
- The current app uses external Unsplash photography and fictional sample menu items, products, event details, and articles. These are not evidence of OM Freedom Cafe’s actual offerings.

## Product Principles

- Make menu, hours, and location easy to find.
- Prefer owner-confirmed practical information over inconsistent directory listings.
- Do not invent prices, contact details, services, events, products, or testimonials.
- Keep actions clear about whether they provide information, make a booking, or complete a purchase.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The requested site should support keyboard operation, visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
